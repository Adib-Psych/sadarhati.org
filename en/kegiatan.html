Sadar Hati Activities

Latest Activities

Throughout 2025–2026, Sadar Hati has actively carried out education, counseling, testing, and cross-agency advocacy — with a focus on current issues: chemsex, mental health, and support for Children Living with HIV/AIDS (ADHA).

World AIDS Day · 1 Dec 2025

Educational Assistance for ADHA

Coinciding with World AIDS Day, Sadar Hati delivered educational aid — school bags, textbooks, and stationery — to Children Living with HIV/AIDS (ADHA). A commitment so that every child can stay in school and look to the future, whatever health challenges they face.
